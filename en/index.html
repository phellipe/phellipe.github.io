01About

Full-stack engineer, former head of IT.

I’m João Phellipe de Freitas Pinto, a software engineer and full-stack developer based in Mossoró, in the state of Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from UFERSA (2010) and an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the joint UERN/UFERSA graduate program (2013). I’m currently a doctoral student in Education, researching the interface between artificial intelligence, digital technologies and education.

I joined UFERSA in 2012 as an Information Technology Analyst, through a competitive civil-service exam. In SUTIC’s Computer Systems Division (SUTIC is the university’s IT department), I work on UFERSA’s integrated management systems, such as SIGAA and SIPAC: legacy Java code that I maintain and extend with the team. From 2020 to 2024, as Superintendent of ICT, I was responsible for IT across the university’s four campuses: infrastructure, systems, governance, information security and staff.

That architecture and management background is what I bring to full-stack products in TypeScript monorepos: React and React Native on the front end, Node.js and PostgreSQL on the back end, Docker and CI/CD to ship it. I’ve solved the problems that tend to stall teams: real-time video with WebRTC and Jitsi on self-hosted infrastructure, ICP-Brasil digital signatures (Brazil’s national public-key infrastructure), multi-tenant systems isolated with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security, offline-first apps and an AI agent serving real customers on WhatsApp.

In my spare time, I work on River Assault, a vertical shooter inspired by the classic River Raid. It’s written in C99 with SDL2 and features a procedurally generated river, five biomes and a custom audio mixer.

04Work

Products, clients and the public sector.

Products I created, systems I built or modernized for clients, and institutional systems I worked on. Client systems are under NDA, so I describe the work by industry, without naming brands.

My own products

  1. 01

    Maintenance — healthcare equipment maintenance

    Own product · in production

    A SaaS I built and run for maintaining hospital, clinical and diagnostic imaging equipment. Any staff member opens a ticket by scanning the device’s QR code with their phone, with no login and no app to install. The technician records the work order on the phone, even offline, with a checklist, photos and a signature. Managers track SLAs, preventive maintenance and calibrations, with a TV dashboard and PDF and Excel reports. It is live at a pilot hospital and is multi-tenant: each institution sees only its own data.

    • React (PWA)
    • Node.js
    • PostgreSQL
    • Docker
    • Service Worker

    Learn about Maintenance (maintenance.app.br, in Portuguese) (opens in a new tab) 

  2. 02

    Quanto Sobra — personal finance

    Own product · Google Play

    A personal finance app that answers one question: how much is left at the end of the month? It works offline: data is stored in SQLite on the phone and syncs with the server. The business logic lives in a pure TypeScript package with 311 automated tests.

    • React Native
    • Expo
    • SQLite
    • Drizzle
    • TypeScript

    Quanto Sobra on Google Play (opens in a new tab)  Quanto Sobra website (in Portuguese) (opens in a new tab) 

  3. 03

    Razão — financial ERP

    Own product

    A financial ERP for small and mid-sized businesses: chart of accounts, income statement, cash flow, sales, commissions and inventory. It is multi-company, with data isolated by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security. It has a TypeScript SDK generated from the OpenAPI spec and a bank-statement import that detects ten banks’ layouts from the file header.

    • Next.js
    • NestJS
    • Drizzle
    • PostgreSQL (RLS)
  4. 04

    Extrajobs — services marketplace

    Own product

    Connects clients with service providers: job posts, proposals, hiring, two-way reviews and real-time chat over Socket.io. It has an API, a website and a mobile app, and the backend has more than 130 automated tests.

    • React
    • React Native
    • Express
    • Prisma
    • Socket.io
  5. 05

    ProntVet — veterinary electronic health record

    Own product

    An electronic health record for veterinary clinics and hospitals: scheduling, SOAP clinical notes with vital signs, structured lab results with reference ranges and alerts for abnormal values, record signing with a publicly verifiable SHA-256 hash, PDF and DOCX export and a pet-owner portal with expiring links (30 days by default).

    • React
    • Node.js
    • PostgreSQL
    • Docker
